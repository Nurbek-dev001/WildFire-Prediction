    What the map shows

    • Each coloured square is one equal-area grid cell. Nothing is interpolated.
    • Blank cells had a detection in the previous 48 hours, so they are not in the new-event risk set that day.
    • Driver bars are additive contributions to the model's uncalibrated log-odds. They describe what the model associates with higher or lower hazard, not causes.
    • ✕ marks cells where a new fire was first detected that day.
