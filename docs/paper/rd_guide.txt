Results and Discussion 
1. Present the main results. Describe the most important findings clearly and directly. 
Example: Pollutant concentrations increased during periods of low temperature and low wind 
speed. 
2. Use figures and tables. Support the findings with relevant figures, tables, or numerical values. 
Figure: Shows trends, patterns, or relationships. 
Table: Presents exact numerical values. 
 Example: Figure 3 shows that NO₂ concentrations were generally higher when the temperature 
was below 0°C. 
3. Explain the results. Describe what the results mean and why the observed patterns may have 
occurred.  
Explain: What does the result mean? Why might this pattern occur? Does it answer the research 
question? Was the result expected? 
Example: Low temperatures may increase pollution levels because of greater heating emissions 
and reduced atmospheric mixing. 
4. Compare with previous studies. Explain whether the findings agree with or differ from earlier 
research. 
 Example: This finding agrees with previous studies reporting that weak winds contribute to 
pollutant accumulation in urban areas. 
5. Discuss the importance. State the scientific or practical value of the findings. 
    Example: The results can help identify weather conditions associated with increased air-pollution 
and health risks in Almaty. 
6. Mention the limitations. Identify the main limitations of the data, method, or study design. 
 Examples: limited observation period, missing measurements, small number of monitoring 
stations, unmeasured environmental factors, limitations of the selected method 
7. Summarise the findings. End the section with a short statement that answers the research 
objective. 
 Example: The results indicate that low temperature and weak wind conditions contribute to 
pollutant accumulation. The proposed method successfully identified the main pollution patterns, 
although further validation using longer observation periods is required. 
Simple structure: Finding + Evidence + Explanation + Comparison + Importance
