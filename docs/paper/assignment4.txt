Assignment 4 - Results, Discussion, and Conclusion 
In this assignment, you will complete the next major sections of your research article: the Results and 
Discussion (can be written separately or merged into one section) and the Conclusion. You will 
continue working in your Overleaf project from Assignments 1–3, following the same formatting 
template (IEEE or Springer) and the same group that you worked before. 
 
Instructions 
1. Use the same LaTeX document from previous assignments. 
2. Add the following sections: 
o Results (or Results and Discussion): 
▪ Present the findings of your research. 
▪ You may use imaginary (“dumb”) data, simulated results, or adapted data 
from similar studies (with clear citation). 
▪ Include tables, graphs, or figures to illustrate trends or comparisons where 
possible. 
▪ Briefly describe how results relate to your research questions or 
hypotheses. 
o Discussion: 
▪ Interpret your results in light of previous research. 
▪ Explain possible reasons for your findings - whether they confirm, contradict, 
or extend existing knowledge. 
▪ Mention limitations or unexpected outcomes. 
▪ Cite relevant work used for comparison. 
o Conclusion: 
▪ Summarize your main findings and their implications. 
▪ Highlight what your study contributes to the field. 
▪ Suggest possible directions for future research. 
 
Deliverables 
• A well-structured document in LaTeX including: 
o Results and Discussion (1200–1500 words) 
o Conclusion (300–400 words) 
• Properly formatted figures, tables, and citations using BibTeX. 
• PDF output uploaded to the submission portal.
